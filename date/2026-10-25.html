Generated by thinkingmachines/Inkling-Small Cinematic video generation requires both temporal inference from limited inputs (i.e., sparse rather than frame-by-frame guidance) and factor-aware control of character, scene, action, and camera. Recent digital agents test this idea by rewriting their own code and checking each version with executable tests.
